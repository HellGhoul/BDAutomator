✅ **Core Data Storage**
- Persistent account data
- Automation logs
- Configuration settings

## **🚀 What Happens When You Launch**

1. **App opens** with RPG-themed interface
2. **Accounts tab** shows your warriors
3. **Automation tab** controls game automation
4. **Settings tab** configures app behavior
5. **WebKit automation** runs in background
6. **Real-time logs** show automation progress
